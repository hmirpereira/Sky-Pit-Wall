About

Sky Pit Wall is a personal fork of WeatherStar 4000+ International by @mwood77, itself based on ws4kp by @netbymatt: a simulation of The Weather Channel's WeatherStar 4000. Not affiliated with The Weather Channel, Formula 1, the FIA, the Lufthansa Group or any team or airline.
